Privacy Policy – Hira Card

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Hira Card ("we", "our", or "us") is a free Android app that helps diamond polishing workers (karigar) track their daily earnings, expenses, and card history. This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected and how it is used.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

All data you enter in the app (earnings, expenses, categories, your name) is stored only on your device using a local database. This data is never uploaded to any server by us.

2. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect your Advertising ID and device information to serve personalized ads. You can manage your ad preferences in the app under Settings → Ad Preferences / Consent.

AdMob Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Firebase Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used (e.g., which screens are visited). This data is anonymous and aggregated. No personally identifiable information is collected.

Firebase Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

3. Advertising ID

With your consent, the app uses your device's Advertising ID (provided by Google) to show relevant ads. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings under Google → Ads.

4. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties, except as described above (AdMob and Firebase, which are Google services).

6. Data Security

Your earnings and expense data lives only on your phone. We recommend enabling your device's screen lock to protect this data.

7. Your Rights (GDPR – EU/EEA Users)

If you are in the EU or EEA, you have the right to:

  • Know what data is collected about you
  • Withdraw consent for personalized ads at any time (Settings → Ad Preferences / Consent)
  • Request deletion of any data held by Google services (via Google's account settings)

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
